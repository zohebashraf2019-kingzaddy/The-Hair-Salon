ABOUT THE STUDIO

Two stylists. One shared vision.

Daniella and Melanie are two hairstylists with a shared passion for beautiful hair, luxury experiences and helping every client feel confident in their own skin.

With 4–5 years of beauty-industry experience for Daniella and 2–3 years for Melanie, they built their careers behind the chair creating effortless, customized looks. They specialize in blondes, brunettes, precision haircuts and extensions, always taking the time to create hair that feels uniquely you.

After growing their clientele in a salon, they opened their own luxury suite. The space reflects their style, culture and passion, with a goal of making every guest feel at home while creating the hair they have been dreaming of.

“My hair bestie and I have opened our own suite and are so excited to have you guys come check it out!”

GOOD TO KNOW

Studio policies.

Deposit

A non-refundable $60 deposit is required to secure qualifying appointments and goes toward your service.

Grace Period

A 15-minute grace period is allowed. After that, the appointment may be forfeited.

Cancellation

Cancellations or reschedules made less than 12 hours before the appointment may be charged 100% of the scheduled service.

No Shows

No-call, no-shows may be charged 100% of the scheduled service.

Refunds

Payments for completed services are final and non-refundable.

Feedback

If you are not totally in love with your hair, please reach out. Your feedback is welcomed so adjustments can be discussed.
